Adaptive energy band [E_min, E_max] -- equations for PowerPoint
(Insert -> Equation -> LaTeX mode, paste one line, Convert)

Energy
E=\frac{1}{2}\dot{q}^T M(q)\dot{q}+\frac{1}{2}(q_v-q)^T K_p(q_v-q)

Soft repulsion (d = penetration depth)
\rho_s=\frac{K_{p,i}\,d}{\delta_s f_d}

E_min: delta_s grows
\dot{\delta}_s=\kappa_{\delta_s}\max(E_{min}-E,\,0)

E_max tightened: delta_s shrinks
\dot{\delta}_s=\kappa_{\delta_s}^{\downarrow}\min(E_{max}-E,\,0),\qquad \delta_s\ge\delta_i

Latch on / off
on:  E_{max}^{+}<E_{max}^{-}\ \wedge\ E>E_{max}^{+}
off: E+\varepsilon_E<E_{max}

DSM energy term
\Delta_E=\max\left(\kappa_s d_{soft},\ \kappa_E(\bar{E}_{max}-E)\right)
\bar{E}_{max}=E_{max}^{-}  while latched,  E_{max}  otherwise

ERG update
\dot{q}_v=\Delta(q,\dot{q},q_v)\,\rho(q_v,q_r)

Notation: E_max^- = E_max before the drop, E_max^+ = after.
